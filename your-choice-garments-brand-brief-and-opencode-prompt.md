> **Purpose:** This document is the single source of truth for planning
> and building the Your Choice Garments website and its associated
> digital catalogue for the Products of PSG Products Expo 2026 in
> Coimbatore.
>
> **Primary commercial objective:** Generate qualified wholesale and
> manufacturing enquiries. Retail/store browsing is secondary.
>
> **Important:** This is a working brief. Any item marked **VERIFY
> BEFORE PUBLISHING** must be confirmed by the business owner. Do not

## 1. Project overview

Build a professional, multi-page website for **Your Choice Garments**, a
Tiruppur-based garment manufacturing and wholesale business. The website
must serve buyers who need blank garments, bulk supply, custom apparel,
private-label products and related garment sourcing.

The site is being prepared initially for an exhibition. It must work
exceptionally well on mobile phones because visitors will scan QR codes
at the stall and browse the catalogue on their own devices.

The website should be designed so it can later move to a permanent
domain and grow into a full ecommerce/B2B ordering platform. For the
current phase, prioritise product discovery, catalogue viewing, WhatsApp
enquiries and lead capture. Do not implement payment checkout unless
specifically requested later.

### Primary success measures

1.  Visitors understand within a few seconds that Your Choice Garments
    is a garment manufacturer / wholesale supplier.
2.  Buyers can quickly find the garment type they need.
3.  Buyers can inspect useful product specifications without guessing.
4.  Buyers can send a structured WhatsApp enquiry for a particular
    product.
5.  Buyers can request a quotation for bulk, custom or private-label
    requirements.
6.  The company appears credible through genuine business information,
    authentic product/factory images and verifiable credentials.
7.  The website captures useful enquiry information and makes follow-up
    practical.

### Target audiences

-   Clothing brands and emerging fashion labels.
-   Apparel retailers and multi-brand stores.
-   Wholesalers, stockists and resellers.
-   Corporate and institutional buyers.
-   Event, promotional merchandise and uniform buyers.
-   Private-label and white-label buyers.
-   Individual retail customers browsing available HAC-designed
    products, where relevant.

The primary audience is B2B wholesale and manufacturing buyers. Retail
customers must not dominate the navigation, homepage messaging or
primary calls to action.

## Design direction

Create a premium, clean, confident manufacturing-company website.

Your Choice Garments identity: - Navy / blue-led corporate palette
derived from the supplied YC logo. - White and light-grey backgrounds. -
Small controlled red accents sampled from the logo. - Modern sans-serif
typography. - Real garment and production photography. - Strong
information hierarchy and spacious layouts. - Clear manufacturing and
wholesale messaging.

The visual quality should feel comparable to a modern, well-designed
garment manufacturer website, with polished responsive behaviour and
consistent spacing. Do not make it look like a generic template or a
streetwear storefront.

HAC is a separate fashion visual identity: - Burgundy / wine and warm
ivory. - Elegant editorial serif plus clean sans-serif. - Use the
supplied HAC logo and "Unleash Your Fashion" tagline. - Keep it visually
secondary to Your Choice Garments on the manufacturing site. - Do not
assert a legal ownership relationship until confirmed.

## Website pages

Build: 1. Home. 2. About Us. 3. Products catalogue. 4. Reusable product
detail pages. 5. Manufacturing. 6. Customisation. 7. Wholesale. 8. Store
(catalogue and enquiry only in the first release). 9. HAC brand feature
/ page, only with approved relationship wording. 10. Contact.

Create a clean global header, dropdown navigation, mobile menu, footer
and persistent enquiry access.

## Homepage requirements

The first screen must communicate: - Your Choice Garments. - Tiruppur
garment manufacturing. - Wholesale / bulk / custom apparel. - A clear
"Explore Products" action. - A clear "Request a Quote" action.

Then show: - Product categories. - Manufacturing overview. -
Customisation capabilities. - Verified business trust information. -
Genuine product / production photography. - Secondary HAC
designed-apparel feature. - Wholesale enquiry CTA.

## Catalogue and product enquiry

Create a searchable and filterable product catalogue with reusable
product cards and detail pages.

Candidate products: - Round neck T-shirts. - Regular fit T-shirts. -
Oversized T-shirts. - Polo T-shirts. - Crop fit T-shirts. - Full sleeve
T-shirts. - Sweatshirts. - Hoodies. - Acid wash garments. - Polyester /
performance T-shirts, if confirmed. - Kids' T-shirts, if confirmed. -
Custom printed T-shirts.

Use only confirmed product facts. Do not invent GSM, material, price,
MOQ, sizes, colour availability, production capacity or delivery
timelines.

Every product must have a prominent **Enquire on WhatsApp** action that
generates a prefilled message containing the product name/code and
fields for quantity, fabric, sizes, colours, customisation and delivery
location.

## Wholesale conversion

The Wholesale page is the most important conversion page.

It must explain: - Who the business serves. - Blank garment supply. -
Bulk garment orders. - Custom apparel. - Private-label possibilities,
only if confirmed. - Product categories. - How the quotation process
works. - What information the buyer should provide. - How to contact the
company.

Add a concise wholesale enquiry form that can hand the completed enquiry
to WhatsApp. Make it easy to use on a mobile phone.

## Trust and proof

Use the IndiaMART TrustSeal image and link to the official verification
profile. Present it accurately as IndiaMART TrustSeal verification. Do
not call it an ISO, quality, export or government certification.

Use authentic factory / product images from the owner. If some images
are missing, create elegant placeholders labelled for the owner to
replace; do not generate fake factory evidence.

Only show approved client names, brand logos and collaborations.

## Store

Make a lightweight store / product browsing experience for single-piece
or designed apparel where approved. Use WhatsApp enquiries. Do not build
a nonfunctional cart, checkout or payment flow in this phase.

## Important restrictions

Do not fabricate: - Company experience claims. - Factory ownership. -
Workforce / capacity / machine figures. - Client names or
testimonials. - Certifications. - Export reach. - Prices / MOQs / GSM /
material specs. - Delivery commitments. - HAC legal ownership or
trademark registration.

When information is unavailable, show a neutral "Enquire for details"
message and keep the data editable.

## Implementation workflow

1.  Audit the repository.
2.  Present the page structure and component plan.
3.  Establish the design tokens.
4.  Implement the global shell.
5.  Build the homepage.
6.  Build the catalogue and product detail template.
7.  Build wholesale and enquiry flows.
8.  Build the remaining pages.
9.  Add SEO and metadata.
10. Test all links, forms and responsive breakpoints.
11. Fix every overflow, overlap, broken image, console error and dead
    interaction.
12. Provide a final summary of files changed, routes created,
    outstanding owner confirmations and deployment steps.

Do not stop after producing a visual mockup. Implement the working
website in the existing project.
